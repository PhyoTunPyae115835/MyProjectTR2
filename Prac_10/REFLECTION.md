## Estimates

Regarding the **estimates** that you did for practical tasks...

### How was your estimate accuracy usually?

My estimates were often slightly optimistic at first. I tended to underestimate how long it would take to fix bugs or understand new concepts.

### How did your estimate accuracy improve or change during the course of the subject?

I became more aware of the time I needed for testing and debugging.

### What did you learn from doing these estimates?

I learned that estimating helps with planning and staying on task. It also helped me notice where I spend too much time.
